# v6.6.0

- **Released:** 2026-11-21
- **Summary:** CMS editors can replace, resize, align and remove photos inside rich text, from a photo bar shown when an image is selected.

## New Features

- Clicking a photo in the rich text editor outlines it and shows a photo bar: Replace (keeps size, alignment and position), Size (small, medium, full width), alignment for small and medium photos (left, centered, right) and Remove from the page. The Delete key still works
- Small is about a third of the column, medium 60%. Left and right photos have the text wrapping around them from tablets up. On phones every photo is full width
- Stored as `data-size` and `data-align` on the image. The sanitizer only keeps known values, and public pages get a matching `sizes` attribute

## Upgrade Notes

No breaking changes, no migration. Photos saved before have neither attribute and stay full width.
