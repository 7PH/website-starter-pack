# v6.4.0

- **Released:** 2026-11-19
- **Summary:** CMS editors set a photo's framing right from the image field, where they pick it, instead of only in Admin > Photos.

## New Features

- The CMS image field (quick edit and full page editor) shows the photo as the focal point picker, with its hint and crop previews. A click saves the framing on the photo right away, and a line says it applies everywhere the photo is used. The page's own Save isn't involved
- New Portrait crop preview next to Square and Wide, in the field and in Admin > Photos

## New Core Files

- `app/frontend/components/cms/CmsFocalEditor.vue` (focal picker, hint and crop previews, shared by the image field and the Admin > Photos panel)

## Upgrade Notes

No breaking changes, no migration. 4 new core strings (en/fr) under `cms.media`.
