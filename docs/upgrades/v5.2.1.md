## Bug Fixes

### Still "not premium" after paying

The subscription status is cached for 30 s per customer, and only webhooks cleared it. The billing tab reads it before checkout, so if the webhook had not arrived yet when the user came back (always the case locally without `stripe listen`), the billing tab and nav kept showing the free plan for up to 30 s. `create_checkout_session` now clears the customer's cached status.
