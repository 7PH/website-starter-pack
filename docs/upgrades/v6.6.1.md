# v6.6.1

- **Released:** 2026-11-21
- **Summary:** Left and right photos in CMS rich text get their gap back, and lists, quotes and centered photos no longer collide with them.

## Bug Fixes

- A left or right photo had no gap with the text beside it: the centering margins beat its side margin. Only centered photos are centered now
- List bullets and quote borders sat on a floated photo. Lists and quotes next to one now start after it
- A centered or full width photo after a floated one was squeezed beside it. It now goes below

## Upgrade Notes

No breaking changes, no migration. Only content with a left or right photo renders differently.
